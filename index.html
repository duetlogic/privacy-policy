SimpleBox Launcher

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 9, 2024

Welcome to SimpleBox Launcher. Your privacy is not just a feature for us; it is a fundamental principle. This policy explains how we handle information in our application.

The Short Version: We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, usage statistics, or technical information. SimpleBox Launcher is built to be completely private and offline.

1. Data Collection

SimpleBox Launcher does not collect any personal information. We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share your name.

Unlike many other launchers, we do not track:

  • Which apps you open or how often you use them.
  • Your location or IP address.
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, etc.).

2. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We do not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools (like Firebase or Google Analytics), or advertising networks. No data is ever sent to our servers or any third-party servers.

3. Device Permissions

SimpleBox Launcher may request certain Android permissions to function correctly (such as accessing the list of installed apps or setting wallpaper). These permissions are used exclusively on your device to provide the user interface. None of the data accessed via these permissions is ever uploaded or shared.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. However, our core philosophy of "zero data collection" will remain unchanged. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
